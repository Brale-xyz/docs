title: "Branded Transfers"
description: "Show your business or your customer's verified identity instead of Brale on ACH transfers and outbound domestic wire payouts."
---

We've expanded the Transfers endpoint to support both 2nd and 3rd party ACH transfers and outbound domestic wire payouts.

When Brale initiates a transfer on behalf of your business or your customer's business, 2nd/3rd-party branding ensures the **receiver sees the intended business name** instead of "Brale". On ACH, `brand` controls the name on the receiver's bank statement line item. On outbound wires, `brand` selects the sender (originator) of the wire.

Branded transfers are supported for ach_debit, ach_credit, same_day_ach_credit, and outbound wire payouts (`destination.transfer_type: "wire"`). Branding is not supported for `rtp_credit` or `fednow_credit`.

To learn more and see a demo, schedule a meeting to [talk to sales](https://brale.xyz/contact).

## Creating 2nd/3rd Party Transfers branded transfers

**POST** `https://api.brale.xyz/accounts/account_id/transfers`

Use the `brand` object to control the business name displayed to the receiver. Pass the target account inside the request body under `brand.account_id`. The `brand.account_id` is the **Account whose name should appear** to the receiver.

- `account_id: aB3hJkL9mN2PqRsTuvWxyz1234` = `Company 1`
  - Your business 
- `account_id: 34fcsGrCgN0Z9zC1v55eP0I6AKW` = `Company 2`
  - Your customer

If you omit `brand`, the transfer uses your default branding.

`brand` and `ach_description` are independent. `brand` controls who the ACH payment is from. `ach_description` controls the short description shown after the sender name on the recipient's bank statement. Set it as `destination.ach_description` for `ach_credit` and `same_day_ach_credit` payouts, or `source.ach_description` for `ach_debit`. See [What recipients see on ACH](/key-concepts/transfers#what-recipients-see-on-ach) for character limits and defaults.

> **Definitions**
>
> - **2nd-party**: You are the brand (display name = your business).
> - **3rd-party**: Your customer's business is the brand (display name = your customer).

### Example of a 2nd Party Transfer (brand = your business)

**POST** `https://api.brale.xyz/accounts/account_id/transfers`

`brand.account_id` = `aB3hJkL9mN2PqRsTuvWxyz1234` (Company 1)

The receiver will see **Company 1** as the name on the ach_debit transaction.

### Confirm the brand on a transfer

Transfer responses include a `brand` field. When the transfer was branded, `brand` is `{ "account_id": "<ACCOUNT_ID>" }`. When it was not branded, `brand` is `null`. The create, get, and list transfer endpoints all return this field, so you can confirm from the response that your brand was applied to an ACH or wire transfer.

A transfer created with `brand` in the request can still return `brand: null`. In that case, Brale sent the transfer unbranded.

## Branded outbound wires

You can brand an outbound domestic wire payout with the same top-level `brand` object. `brand.account_id` selects the verified Account identity used as the wire sender (originator). It is not free-form display-name text.

A branded wire request involves three different parties:

| Field | What it selects |
| :---- | :-------------- |
| `account_id` in the URL | The Account that funds the transfer. `source.address_id` must be a funded internal Address on this Account. |
| `brand.account_id` | The Account whose verified identity appears as the wire sender. This can be your business (2nd-party) or your customer's business (3rd-party). |
| `destination.address_id` | The recipient's wire-capable bank Address. |

Branding only changes the sender identity on the wire. It does not change which Account funds the transfer, who owns the source or destination, custody, or the existing payout eligibility and verification requirements. If you omit `brand`, the wire is sent unbranded, as before. You do not need to create an Automation or set up a separate funding account to send a branded wire.

`brand` and `destination.wire_memo` are independent. `brand` controls who the wire is from. `destination.wire_memo` controls the payment reference text sent with the wire. Pass an explicit `wire_memo` when the recipient needs a specific reference.

### Example of a branded wire payout (Company 2 = your customer)

**POST** `https://api.brale.xyz/accounts/{account_id}/transfers`

In this example, your business account in the URL funds the payout from its internal Address, and the wire is sent with **Company 2** as the sender.

```json Request
{
  "amount": {
    "value": "2500.00",
    "currency": "USD"
  },
  "source": {
    "address_id": "2VcUIonJeVQzFoBuC7LdFT0dRe4",
    "value_type": "SBC",
    "transfer_type": "solana"
  },
  "destination": {
    "address_id": "2AbCdEfGHiJkLmN0pQrStUvWxYz",
    "value_type": "USD",
    "transfer_type": "wire",
    "wire_memo": "Invoice 1048"
  },
  "brand": {
    "account_id": "34fcsGrCgN0Z9zC1v55eP0I6AKW"
  }
}
```

- `source.address_id` is a funded internal Address on the Account in the URL. `source.value_type` and `source.transfer_type` match the token and network it holds.
- `destination.address_id` is an external bank Address with `wire` in its `transfer_types`.
- `destination.transfer_type` is `wire` and `destination.value_type` is `USD`.

Creating the Transfer does not mean the wire has settled. Processing a branded wire can take longer while Brale prepares the brand Account's sender identity. Keep tracking the original Transfer while it is `pending` instead of creating a replacement, and reuse the same `Idempotency-Key` if you retry the same request.

See [Stablecoin to fiat offramp](/guides/stablecoin-to-fiat-offramp) to create the recipient's wire-capable bank Address and to track the payout with `destination.payment_details.imad`, returns, and refunds.

## Brand account errors

When you create a branded transfer, Brale can reject the request if the Account in `brand.account_id` can't be used as a brand:

| Status | `id`                            | What to do                                                                                                   |
| ------ | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `404`  | `brand_account_not_found`       | Check that `brand.account_id` is correct.                                                                    |
| `422`  | `brand_account_not_verified`    | Contact support to resolve the brand Account before retrying.                                                |
| `422`  | `brand_account_update_required` | Contact support to resolve the brand Account. Then retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` and body.           |
| `503`  | `brand_identity_unavailable`    | Retry the same request with the same `Idempotency-Key` after a few minutes. Contact support if it continues. |

Brale does not store the `422 brand_account_update_required` or `503 brand_identity_unavailable` response for the `Idempotency-Key`. The same request succeeds once support fixes the brand Account or the service recovers.
