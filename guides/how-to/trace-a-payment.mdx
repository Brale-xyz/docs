Use this guide when a sender or recipient asks where their funds are, or when a bank asks for a reference to trace a payment. Each fiat rail uses a different reference. Brale exposes some references on the Transfer. Brale support can provide the others.

## Prerequisites

- Your `account_id`: [How to get your account_id](/guides/how-to/get-your-account-id)
- The `id` of the Transfer you want to trace. Use the Transfer that moved fiat, not a related onchain mint, burn, or swap. For example, a USDC-funded mint has no wire IMAD.

## Find the reference for each rail

[Retrieve the Transfer](/api-reference/brale/get-transfer) and read the field for the rail and direction.

```bash
curl --request GET \
  --url "https://api.brale.xyz/accounts/${ACCOUNT_ID}/transfers/${TRANSFER_ID}" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer ${AUTH_TOKEN}"
```

| Rail | Direction | Reference | Where to find it | When it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wire | Inbound | IMAD | `source.payment_details.imad` | When Brale receives the wire |
| Wire | Outbound | IMAD | `destination.payment_details.imad` | After the sending bank submits the wire. It can appear after the Transfer is created or reaches `complete`. |
| Wire | Outbound | OMAD | Not exposed in the API. Request it from support. | After the receiving bank acknowledges the wire |
| ACH | Inbound | ACH trace number | `source.payment_details.trace_number` | When the ACH payment posts |
| ACH (`ach_credit`, `same_day_ach_credit`) | Outbound | ACH trace number | `destination.payment_details.trace_number` | When the payout posts |
| RTP (`rtp_credit`) | Outbound | Depends on the rail used. See [RTP and FedNow payouts](#rtp-and-fednow-payouts). | Request it from support | After the payout settles |
| FedNow (`fednow_credit`) | Inbound and outbound | End-to-end ID | Not exposed in the API. Request it from support. | After the payment settles |

`payment_details` is optional. If the object or field is absent, Brale does not have the bank metadata yet. Retrieve the Transfer again later. See [`source.payment_details`](/key-concepts/transfers#sourcepayment_details) and [`destination.payment_details`](/key-concepts/transfers#destinationpayment_details) for the field reference.

## IMAD and OMAD for outbound wires

A Fedwire payment has two references:

- **IMAD** (Input Message Accountability Data) is assigned when Brale's bank sends the wire. Brale returns it in `destination.payment_details.imad`.
- **OMAD** (Output Message Accountability Data) is assigned when the Federal Reserve delivers the wire to the receiving bank. It confirms that the receiving bank got the wire.

The Brale API does not return the OMAD. To get it, email [support@brale.xyz](mailto:support@brale.xyz) with the Transfer `id`.

Give the recipient the IMAD first. Their bank can use it to locate the incoming wire. If the receiving bank returns the wire, Brale moves the Transfer to `failed` with `failure.type: "wire_return"`. See [Returned outbound wires](/guides/stablecoin-to-fiat-offramp#returned-outbound-wires).

## RTP and FedNow payouts

Wire fields such as `imad` and ACH fields such as `trace_number` do not apply to FedNow. Instant payments are traced with an end-to-end ID, which the Brale API does not return. To get it, email [support@brale.xyz](mailto:support@brale.xyz) with the Transfer `id`.

An `rtp_credit` payout does not always settle over the RTP network:

- Brale can send an `rtp_credit` payout over FedNow. The reference is a FedNow end-to-end ID.
- If your organization enabled [RTP fallback](/documentation/platform/transfer-timing#rtp-fallback-to-same-day-ach), a failed RTP payout can settle over Same Day ACH. The reference is an ACH trace number, and Same Day ACH timing applies.

When you ask support for a reference, support tells you which rail the payout used.

## Inbound international (SWIFT) wires

A sender outside the US can send USD by SWIFT to an [Automation's](/guides/fiat-to-stablecoin-onramp#fiat-to-stablecoin-automation-automatic-onramp) wire funding instructions. The payment routes through one or more intermediary banks before it reaches Brale's bank, where it arrives as a wire. Brale creates a Transfer only after the wire arrives.

International wires take longer than domestic wires. There is no fixed arrival time, and some corridors take several business days. A sender's receipt that shows the payment as initiated does not mean it has reached Brale's bank.

If an international wire does not appear, ask the sender for the **UETR** (Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference) from their bank. Email it to [support@brale.xyz](mailto:support@brale.xyz) with the amount, the send date, and the Automation `id`. Brale can use the UETR to trace the payment with its bank.

## Originator name on payouts

The name the recipient's bank shows for a payout depends on the rail:

- **ACH:** Optional statement branding applies to ACH payouts only. See [Branding](/documentation/api-design/primitives#branding).
- **Wire, RTP, and FedNow:** Statement branding does not apply. Wire payouts are sent with Brale Inc. as the originator.

## Get proof of payment

Use the Transfer as your record of the payout. Share these values with the sender or recipient:

- The Transfer `id`, `status`, `amount`, and `updated_at`
- The rail reference from the [table above](#find-the-reference-for-each-rail)

If the recipient's bank needs more than the rail reference, email [support@brale.xyz](mailto:support@brale.xyz) with the Transfer `id`. Include the reference you already have and what the bank asked for.

## Before you contact support

1. Confirm the Transfer `status`. A `pending` or `processing` Transfer is still in progress. See [Transfer timing](/documentation/platform/transfer-timing) for each rail's expected settlement time.
2. Check `payment_details` for the IMAD or trace number.
3. If the Transfer is `failed`, read the [`failure`](/api-reference/brale/get-transfer#failure) object for return details.
4. Give the recipient's bank the reference. Most banks can locate a payment with the IMAD, trace number, or end-to-end ID.
