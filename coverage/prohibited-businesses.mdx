---
title: "Prohibited businesses"
description: "Business types Brale does not onboard for custodial or transactional accounts."
---

Brale does not onboard the following businesses:

- Illegal gambling
- Pseudo-pharmaceuticals
- Debt-related services
- Weapons vendors/manufacturers
- Hate groups or any material that promotes violence or hatred
- Counterfeit goods / IP infringement (hacker groups or theft)
- Illegal/controlled substances vendors
- Drug paraphernalia
- Missions, embassies and consulates
- Adult content/services & classifieds
- Multilevel marketing / predatory lending / predatory investment schemes
- Any licensed activity where the provider is operating without a license, or illegal sale of cryptocurrency to consumers
- Offshore banks and companies with no physical address and no purpose (shell corporations)

These prohibitions apply to both custodial and transactional accounts.

<Note>
A business that is not on this list is not guaranteed approval. All account creation remains subject to verification, program approval, and applicable compliance requirements.
</Note>

For questions about whether a business is eligible, contact [support@brale.xyz](mailto:support@brale.xyz).
